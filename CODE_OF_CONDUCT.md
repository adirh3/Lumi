# Code of Conduct

Everyone taking part in Lumi's repository, issues, pull requests and reviews,
including the maintainer, is expected to follow these standards.

## Participate respectfully

- Be welcoming to people with different backgrounds, experience and viewpoints.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about ideas and code, not personal attacks.
- Respect people's time, boundaries and privacy. Do not publish someone else's
  personal information or private conversations without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten or demean people, use hateful language, direct unwanted
  sexual attention at them, or flood discussions with spam.

Good-faith technical disagreement, bug reports and AI-assisted contributions are
not misconduct by themselves. Contributions must still follow
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), including its rules for human ownership and
automated submissions.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the repository maintainer privately at
**[support@fluentsearch.net](mailto:support@fluentsearch.net)** with the subject
**`[Lumi conduct]`**. Include a concise description and relevant issue or comment
links. Share only the context needed to understand the concern, and redact
credentials and unrelated personal or confidential information.

Do not publish sensitive report details in a public issue. For a software
vulnerability rather than a conduct concern, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

The repository maintainer handles reports and moderation. Depending on the
context, impact and pattern of behavior, the maintainer may request changes,
remove inappropriate content, close or limit discussions, or restrict
participation temporarily or permanently. Serious or repeated violations may
lead to restrictions without a prior warning.

You can ask for clarification or reconsideration using the same private
contact. No fixed response or resolution timeline is promised.
